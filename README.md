# 🚀 Thumba to Space - From Thiruvananthapuram to the Stars

**Live Demo:** Coming soon on Hack Club Nest `mireya-6078`
**Stardance Project:** NASA + Hack Club Summer 2026

### What is it?
An interactive web experience that tells India's space story - starting from the small fishing village of Thumba, Kerala (near magnetic equator) to Chandrayaan-3 Moon landing and beyond.

Built for NASA Stardance Challenge 2026, this site combines ISRO history with live NASA data.

### Why I Built It?
I am from Kilimanoor, Kerala - close to Thumba where ISRO started in 1963. I wanted to show our journey in a fun, visual way for students.

Thumba was chosen because it's near Earth's magnetic equator - perfect for sounding rockets. From a church building to Mars!

### Features
- 🚀 Thumba 1963 - The Beginning
- 🌙 Chandrayaan Missions
- 🛰️ Live ISS Tracker (NASA API)
- 🌌 NASA Astronomy Picture of the Day
- ⏳ Gaganyaan Countdown - 15 Sep 2026
- IN - ISRO & NASA collaboration
